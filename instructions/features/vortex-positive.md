# Vortex Positive

## Definition

Positive Vortex movement / summed true range

## Measurement and timing

**Unit and scale:** dimensionless ratio

**Historical lookback:** 14 trading observation(s)

**Publication lag (trading sessions):** 0

Lookbacks count trading observations, not calendar days or forecast sessions. Rolling windows include the current row unless the definition explicitly uses prior rows. Nominal lookback is not a convergence warmup guarantee.

## Interpretation notes

- Interpret the ratio using its stated numerator and denominator. A value of 1 is equality of those quantities, not automatically a neutral market signal.
- Use Positive and Negative together; the spread states their difference. A direction flag gives the comparison result without its magnitude.
- Catalog lag 0 means no extra publication shift is specified. It does not establish that a supplied session bar is complete or that data are current.
